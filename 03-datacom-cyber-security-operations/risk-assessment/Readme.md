## Additional Task: Cybersecurity Risk Assessment for RetailNova Pty Ltd

I completed a cybersecurity risk assessment for RetailNova Pty Ltd using a Threat and Vulnerability Identification Template and a Cybersecurity Risk Prioritisation Matrix.

### Tasks Completed

- Identified critical assets, associated threats and potential vulnerabilities.
- Documented mitigation measures for identified security weaknesses.
- Assessed inherent and residual risks using likelihood and consequence ratings.
- Applied a 5x5 risk matrix to prioritise risks as Low, Medium, High or Extreme.
- Identified existing security controls and recommended additional treatments.
- Defined risk ownership, monitoring activities and review requirements.

### Risk Assessment Methodology

The assessment followed a structured approach:

1. Asset identification.
2. Threat and vulnerability identification.
3. Review of existing security controls.
4. Inherent risk assessment before additional controls.
5. Residual risk assessment after controls.
6. Risk treatment, prioritisation and monitoring.

### Risk Prioritisation

Risks were evaluated using a 5x5 matrix based on likelihood and consequence, each rated from 1 to 5.

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| Low | Acceptable with routine controls and monitoring. |
| Medium | Requires management attention and review of additional controls. |
| High | Requires priority treatment and close monitoring. |
| Extreme | Requires immediate escalation and remediation. |

### Key Learning Outcomes

- Threat and vulnerability identification.
- Cybersecurity risk analysis and prioritisation.
- Inherent and residual risk evaluation.
- Security control assessment and mitigation planning.
- Risk ownership, monitoring and reporting.
